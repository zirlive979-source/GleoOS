# GleoOS Privacy & Policy

Version: 1.0.1  
Developer: Dika

This prototype is designed for educational use.

## Local data

The current v1.0.1 kernel does not implement online accounts, cloud
synchronization, analytics, advertising, or a network service.

Future account and application-store features must clearly explain what
information is collected, why it is needed, where it is stored, and how
users can remove it.

## Third-party services

GleoOS does not bundle Google Chrome or Google Play Store in this prototype.
Third-party services, if supported in a future release, remain subject to
their own terms and licenses.

## Security

GleoOS v1.0.1 is an early educational prototype and should not be treated
as a secure production operating system.
